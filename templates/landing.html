Process

How EduPredict Works

Four simple steps from raw data to actionable AI-powered student insights.

01

Data Collection

Students and teachers input academic metrics — attendance, marks, study hours, LMS activity, and more.

02

Feature Processing

7 academic features are extracted, validated, and normalized through the scikit-learn pipeline.

03

ML Prediction

Random Forest Classifier evaluates features and predicts performance tier, risk level, and confidence score.

04

Actionable Insights

Results are saved to the database and displayed through role-specific dashboards with visual analytics.
